# Privacy

OCTORA TV OS has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

**What the app connects to**

- **Your TV**, on your home network, only after you press Allow on the TV. It reads the list of apps and settings it
  needs, and makes the changes you choose.
- **GitHub** (`api.github.com`), at most every few hours, to see whether a newer version of the app exists. This is a
  normal web request; nothing about you or your TV is sent.
- **Optional panels you switch on** (for example weather) are fetched by the TV's home screen from their public
  services. They are off until you turn them on.

**What stays on your phone**

The safety backup of your TV, your choices and your pictures are stored on your phone only. Uninstalling the app
removes them.
